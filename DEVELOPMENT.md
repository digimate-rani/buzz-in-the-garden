# Buzz in the Garden: development notes

A record of how the game was built, how it works inside, what was measured, and where it can go next. Written so a person or a coding agent can pick the project up without the original conversation.

- **Play:** https://digimate-rani.github.io/buzz-in-the-garden/
- **Source of truth:** `src/buzz.html` (one self-contained file, about 1,350 lines of HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no dependencies)
- **Built site:** `docs/` (generated by `build.py`; served by GitHub Pages)
- **UI language:** Hebrew, right to left. Code, comments and commit messages are in English.

## 1. The game in one paragraph

You are a bee in a top-down garden. Drag a finger anywhere on the screen and the bee follows the finger's movement (relative drag, so the finger never covers the bee). Fly through flowers to collect pollen into a pouch. Consecutive pollen of the same color is worth more. Fly back into the hive to bank the pouch as honey, which is the score in grams. Wasps chase you, and a sting drops the whole pouch and costs a heart. Each score target makes a bomb flower bloom; collecting it blows up every wasp and advances to the next of 10 levels, each adding a new twist.

## 2. How it was built (timeline)

The project went through six iterations. Each was driven by playing it on a real phone.

| # | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | First version as a claude.ai Artifact: bee, three flower colors, chain scoring, pouch of 6, hive banking, wasps, 3 hearts, dusk cycle, sounds, haptics, Hebrew UI | Request: "a fun, creative phone game" |
| 2 | First battery pass: stop the loop when paused or hidden, cap at 60fps, lawn on its own static layer, cached glow sprites, no `backdrop-filter`, canvas resolution capped at 2x, release the wake lock | The phone got hot |
| 3 | 10 levels, bomb flower, 10-second hive shelter limit, 12-slot pouch, new colors, spider webs, Oriental hornet, heatwave, night | Request for progression and difficulty |
| 4 | Second battery pass, driven by measurement: sprite rendering for every flower, insect and the hive; battery saver mode (30fps, 1.5x) on by default; audio suspended between rounds | Still hot. Profiling showed drawing, not game logic, was the cost |
| 5 | Offline PWA package: bundled font, manifest, icons, service worker, `build.py`, Caddy and nginx configs, deploy script | Request to play offline, self-hosted on a VPS |
| 6 | This repository with GitHub Pages, plus a root `index.html` that redirects to `docs/` | Request for a public GitHub link |

The full commit history of iterations 1 to 5 also exists on branch `claude/fun-phone-game-jhz9h6` of `digimate-rani/gardening_dash`, if that branch was pushed.

## 3. Game rules (as implemented)

### Scoring
- Pollen value grows along a same-color chain: 10, 20, 30, and so on (`10 × chain length`). A different color restarts the chain at 10.
- A **gold flower** (7% of spawns, 14% on level 10) continues any chain and is worth `20 × chain length`. Gold flowers wilt faster (6 to 8 seconds).
- **Banking** happens instantly on entering the hive: the pouch total is added to the score, then the pouch and chain reset.
- **Pure honey bonus:** banking with a chain of 6 or more adds `150 + 50 × (chain − 6)`. (Originally it required a full pouch of one color; it was changed when the pouch grew to 12 slots and colors grew to 5, because that became nearly impossible.)
- **Extra heart:** every 1,000 grams banked restores a heart, up to 3.

### Pouch
- 6 slots on levels 1 and 2, 12 slots (two HUD rows) from level 3.
- Flying through a flower with a full pouch shows a "go to the hive" message and an arrow points to the hive.
- The collected pollen trails behind the bee as a line of colored balls.

### Hive shelter
- Wasps do not enter the hive while it shelters, and a bee inside cannot be hit.
- Shelter is **10 seconds for the whole game** (`SHELTER_MAX`), as requested. It drains only after the bee has stayed inside for more than 1 second, so passing through to bank costs nothing.
- A ring around the hive shows what is left. A countdown appears while draining. At zero the hive stops protecting.
- The limit is literal: it does not refill on level-up. Making it per level is a one-line change in `collectBomb()` (`shelter = SHELTER_MAX`).

### Hazards
- **Wasps** spawn from the left, right or top edge after a red "!" warning (1.2 seconds, 1.6 for a hornet). They wander, chase the bee inside a reach radius (`240 + level × 14` px), and avoid the hive while it shelters.
- **Oriental hornet** (levels 6 and up): 1.4x size, reddish. It roams, then stops and shows a dashed red line toward the bee for 0.8 seconds, then charges in that fixed direction at 560 px/s for 0.55 seconds. The fixed direction is what makes it dodgeable. (A real species in Israel that preys on honey bees; there is a fact about it on the game-over card.)
- **Spider webs** (levels 4 and up): the bee's follow speed drops from 13 to 2.6 while inside one. They are re-placed on each level-up.
- **Sting:** −1 heart, the pouch is lost (pollen bursts out), 1.8 seconds of invulnerability (the bee blinks), the wasp is knocked back.

### Levels
All tuning lives in the `LEVELS` table near the top of the script. Each entry:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `at` | Score at which this level's bomb flower blooms (from the previous level) |
| `speed: [start, cap]` | Wasp speed in px/s; it rises by 1 px/s per second spent in the level, up to the cap |
| `max` | Most regular wasps on screen |
| `every` | Seconds between new wasp arrivals |
| `colors` | How many of `KINDS` can spawn (3, then 4 orange, then 5 white) |
| `pouch` | Pouch size |
| `webs`, `hornets` | Counts |
| `wilt` | Multiplier on flower lifetime (0.7 = heatwave) |
| `night` | Forces the dusk overlay to near full |
| `gold` | Gold flower chance override |
| `title`, `note` | Text on the level-up banner |

Current values:

| Level | `at` | Wasp speed | Max | Every | New |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 78 to 150 | 5 | 22s | |
| 2 | 5,000 | 108 to 192 | 6 | 14s | faster, more frequent wasps |
| 3 | 8,000 | 114 to 198 | 6 | 13s | 12-slot pouch, orange |
| 4 | 12,000 | 120 to 205 | 7 | 12s | 2 webs |
| 5 | 16,500 | 126 to 212 | 7 | 11s | white |

Wasp arrival: level 1 waits 7 seconds then starts with 1 wasp; later levels wait 3 seconds then start with 2. The bomb flower clears all wasps on screen and the counts restart for the new level.

Difficulty balance has **not** been tuned on a device. These numbers are first guesses.

## 4. Code map (`src/buzz.html`)

The file is plain HTML with one `<style>` block and one IIFE `<script>`. No build step is needed to run it; `build.py` only wraps it for offline use.

### Structure
- **HTML:** `#app` holds two canvases (`#lawn`, static; `#c`, game), a `#dusk` tint layer, the HUD (hearts, score, mute, pause, level bar, pouch), the level-up `#banner`, and three overlay cards: `#menu`, `#pauseOv`, `#over`.
- **CSS:** design tokens on `:root` (lawn greens, honey, ink, petal colors, `--display` font stack). Single deliberate look, no light/dark switch.
- **Script sections**, in order: constants and `LEVELS`; DOM handles and state; helpers; audio; layout (`resize`, `buildBg`); spawning (`spawnFlower`, `setupWebs`, `reset`, `spawnWasp`); game events (`collect`, `bank`, `collectBomb`, `showBanner`, `hornetStep`, `hit`); `update(dt)`; drawing (sprites, `drawHive`, `drawFlower`, `drawInsect`, `render`); HUD (`renderHud`); flow (`start`, `gameOver`, `pause`, `resume`, `toMenu`); input; loop (`idle`, `kick`, `frame`).

### State machine
`state` is one of `menu`, `play`, `pause`, `over`.
- `menu`: the bee flies a demo path behind the menu card; no collisions.
- `play`: full simulation.
- `pause`: nothing updates and the loop stops.
- `over`: particles finish for about a second, then the game-over card shows and the loop stops.

### Main loop
`frame(now)` runs on `requestAnimationFrame` but skips frames to hold a target rate: 30fps in battery saver, 60fps in smooth mode, 20 to 30fps in the menu. `dt` is capped at 0.06 seconds. `idle()` stops the loop entirely when the page is hidden, paused, or showing the game-over card; `kick()` restarts it.

### Entities
- `bee`: position, target (`tx`, `ty`), heading, invulnerability timer, a 50-entry position history used for the trail and the pollen tail.
- `flowers[]`: `kind` (`pink`, `violet`, `blue`, `orange`, `white`, `gold`, `bomb`), position, scale, rotation, age, life, taken state.
- `wasps[]`: position, velocity, warn timer, wander angle, `hornet` flag, hornet `phase` (`roam`, `wind`, `dash`), and `die` (a countdown set by the bomb shockwave by distance).
- `parts[]` (capped at 140) and `floats[]` (capped at 14): particles and floating text.
- `webs[]`, `shock` (the expanding bomb ring).

### Rendering
- The lawn (gradient, mowing stripes, tufts, clover, worn patch near the hive) is drawn once per resize onto `#lawn`.
- Every frame `#c` is cleared and redrawn: hive, flowers, webs, trail, pollen tail, wasps, bee, arrows, particles, floating text, fireflies.
- **Sprite cache** (`sprite(key, size, draw)`): each flower kind, each insect body, the wings, the hive and every floating text label is drawn once into an offscreen canvas at 1.25 × device scale and then stamped with `drawImage`. The cache is cleared on resize. The bomb flower is still drawn live, because it animates.
- The dusk tint is a CSS layer whose opacity changes only when its rounded value changes, and it is hidden when zero.

### Storage (`localStorage`, per device)
`buzz-best`, `buzz-best-level`, `buzz-muted`, `buzz-eco`. Every access is wrapped in `try/catch`, because storage can be unavailable.

## 5. Performance: what was learned

The phone heated up twice. The second time it was measured instead of guessed, with headless Chromium at a phone viewport (390×844 at 3x), on level 6 with webs, a hornet and wasps on screen, using the Chrome DevTools Protocol `Performance.getMetrics` (`TaskDuration` over 8 seconds of simulated play).

| Version | Main thread busy | Script only |
|---|---|---|
| Before the second pass (60fps, 2x, paths every frame) | about 49% | about 4% |
| Battery saver (sprites, 30fps, 1.5x) | about 17% | about 1.3% |
| Smooth mode (sprites, 60fps, 2x) | about 45% | about 2.5% |
| Empty frame (clear only, battery saver) | about 7% | about 0.6% |

Conclusions:
- **Game logic was never the cost.** Rasterizing the full-screen canvas was. Optimizing JavaScript would not have helped.
- **Frame rate and resolution dominate.** Sprites helped, but most of the saving came from 30fps and 1.5x.
- About 7% is the fixed cost of compositing a full-screen canvas at all.
- Inside the Claude app the page runs in an extra webview layer, which adds overhead the page cannot control. The installed PWA avoids it.
- These numbers come from a software-rendering desktop browser. The relative change should carry over to phones; the absolute numbers will not.

Rules to keep when adding features:
- Draw anything static or repeated into a sprite once; never re-trace complex paths per object per frame.
- Avoid per-frame `createRadialGradient`, `shadowBlur`, `filter` and CSS `backdrop-filter` over the canvas.
- Do not create objects or canvases per frame.
- Stop the loop whenever nothing moves.

## 6. Offline app (PWA)

- `build.py` (Python standard library only) reads `src/buzz.html`, removes the Google Fonts links, adds `@font-face` rules for the three bundled Fredoka files (Hebrew, Latin, Latin Extended; one variable file each covers weights 300 to 700), adds the manifest, icon and iOS meta tags, and appends service worker registration. It writes `docs/index.html` and `docs/sw.js`.
- `sw.js` precaches all 11 files on install and answers from the cache first. The cache name is `buzz-` plus a hash of the files' contents, so **any change produces a new cache automatically**; `skipWaiting` and `clients.claim` activate it, and old caches are deleted. A phone gets the update on the next launch with internet and shows it from the launch after.
- `manifest.webmanifest`: standalone display, portrait, Hebrew RTL, theme `#2f5e1e`, icons at 192 and 512 plus a maskable 512.
- Icons were rendered with headless Chromium using the same bee art as the game, on a honeycomb over the lawn.
- **Tested:** served from localhost, loaded once, browser set offline, reloaded. The game loaded from the cache with the bundled font and no external requests, a round started, no console errors.
- **Not tested:** installation on a real iPhone or Android device.

## 7. Deployment

### GitHub Pages (current)
Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → `main` → `/docs`. If the folder is set to `/ (root)` instead, the root `index.html` redirects to `docs/`, and the root `.nojekyll` stops Jekyll from rendering the README as the site.

### Own server (optional)
`deploy/Caddyfile` (checked with `caddy validate`; Caddy obtains HTTPS automatically), `deploy/nginx.conf` (not tested; use certbot for HTTPS), and `deploy/deploy.sh user@host` (runs `build.py`, then `rsync` of `docs/`). HTTPS is mandatory: service workers do not run on plain HTTP except on localhost. The configs send `Cache-Control: no-cache` for `index.html`, `sw.js` and the manifest so updates are always noticed.


### Testing approach used so far
There is no test suite in the repository yet. Testing was done with Playwright scripts outside it:
- **Smoke test:** load, start, drag, check for page errors.
- **Level walk:** a test-only copy of the page injects a hook just before the script's closing `kick();`, for example:
  ```js
  window.__t = {
    setScore: v => { score = v; renderHud(); },
    god: () => { hearts = 999; },
    move: (x, y) => { bee.tx = x; bee.ty = y; },
    get bomb() { const b = flowers.find(f => f.kind === 'bomb' && !f.taken); return b && { x: b.x, y: b.y }; },
    get level() { return level; }
  };
  ```
  The test then sets the score to each threshold, steers the bee onto the bomb flower, and checks the level, wasp count, pouch size, webs and flower kinds.
- **Performance benchmark:** CDP `Performance.getMetrics`, as described in section 5.
- **Offline test:** as described in section 6.

Adding these as `tests/` with a small `package.json` (Playwright) would be a good first step before larger changes.

### Gotchas
- The page relies on `[hidden] { display: none !important; }`. The claude.ai Artifact viewer adds it by default; the file also declares it so it works anywhere.
- Canvas text is pre-rendered with `direction = 'rtl'`. Hebrew strings in the `float()` calls render correctly; check mixed Hebrew and numbers when adding new ones.
- `topSafe()` comes from the HUD's measured height, so spawn areas adapt when the pouch grows to two rows. Call `renderHud()` after any HUD layout change.
- The hit test uses distance between centers: 23 px for wasps and 30 px for hornets.
- `ctx` is reassigned temporarily while drawing sprites; drawing helpers must use the global `ctx`, not a captured reference.

## 9. Known gaps

- Level balance, bomb thresholds and the hive limit are untested on a phone.
- No automated tests in the repository.
- Scores are per device; there is no shared leaderboard.
- No English UI.
- iOS has not been tested at all. The only device seen so far is an Android phone with Chrome.
- Reloading the Artifact or the PWA mid-round loses the round; there is no saved game state.

## 10. Ideas for the next layers

Grouped from cheapest to most involved.

**Polish**
- A settings card: sensitivity of the drag, haptics on or off, battery saver, and a reset of the best score.
- A short first-run tutorial on level 1 that points at a flower, the hive and the first wasp.
- Distinct sounds per flower color, and a soft ambient garden loop with a volume control.
- A level select for levels already reached.

**Gameplay**
- Power-ups that bloom rarely: a lavender flower that slows wasps, a clover that gives a shield sting, nectar that briefly doubles pollen value.
- Combo goals per level ("bank a chain of 8 pink"), with stars per level.
- A queen wasp boss after level 10, then an endless mode with a leaderboard.
- Weather: wind that drifts the bee, rain that closes flowers.
- Day/night tied to the phone's real local time.

**Platform**
- A saved game (`localStorage` snapshot at each bank) so a reload can resume.
- A shared leaderboard. GitHub Pages is static, so this needs a small backend (the VPS with a tiny API and SQLite fits).
- English and Hebrew UI from a string table, with the layout already RTL-aware.
- Achievements and a stats screen.
- Wrap the PWA as an Android app with a Trusted Web Activity (Bubblewrap) for the Play Store; it reuses the same `docs/` site.

**Code health**
- Split `src/buzz.html` into modules (`levels.js`, `render.js`, `entities.js`, `audio.js`) bundled by `build.py`, once the file grows further.
- Move the Playwright scripts into `tests/` and run them in GitHub Actions on every push.
- Measure on a real phone with Chrome remote debugging before and after any rendering change.
